Examples

Runnable scripts in the repository's examples/ folder:

FileWhat it shows
examples/truss-optimization1.jlgeometry optimization with a compliance objective (the Quick start)
examples/truss-optimization2.jlconstrained minimum volume: geometry and sizing, with MMA
examples/truss-optimization3-nlopt.jlthe recommended pattern: NLopt driven directly, a reverse-mode objective, a forward-mode constraint Jacobian and CachedSolver (about 9× faster per run than the all-Zygote formulation)
examples/vierendeel-geometry.jlframe optimization: a Vierendeel truss profile, volume-scaled compliance, hand-rolled projected gradient descent
examples/joint-stiffness.jlJointVariable: the cheapest distribution of semi-rigid connection stiffness that meets a drift limit
examples/ad_backends/the same problem differentiated with Zygote, Mooncake and Enzyme

Engineering reports with full benchmark tables are in the repository's docs/ folder: AD backend comparisons, AD verification against the published implementation, and the publication gb_mma benchmark.
